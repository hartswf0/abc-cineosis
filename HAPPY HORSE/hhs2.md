TITLE: SHE'S JUST A HAPPY HORSE

EXT. ITALIAN PASTURE – DAY

A horse grazes peacefully. DARIO AMODEI sits nearby.

VOICEOVER
Sometimes he looks at her and thinks: she does not know about any of this.

The horse continues eating grass.

CUT TO:

INT. ANTHROPIC BOARDROOM – DAY

Executives discuss AI risk, labor disruption, geopolitics, and existential threats.

VOICEOVER
The man who thinks about civilization finds relief beside a creature untouched by the conversation.

CUT TO:

EXT. OGYGIA – MYTHIC TIME

CALYPSO, the nymph of myth, offers immortality to ODYSSEUS.

VOICEOVER
The name is not innocent.

Odysseus looks toward a distant horizon.

CUT TO:

EXT. ITALIAN PASTURE – DAY

The horse Calypso lifts her head briefly.

VOICEOVER
This Calypso does not trap the traveler. She releases him.

CUT TO:

INT. ANTHROPIC OFFICE – DAY

Safety reports. Alignment diagrams. Evaluation dashboards.

VOICEOVER
Anthropic sells more than software. It sells a temperament.

Documents marked SAFETY, GOVERNANCE, INTERPRETABILITY fill the screen.

CUT TO:

INT. COURTROOM – DAY

Stacks of books. Attorneys argue copyright claims.

VOICEOVER
The law asks an unfashionable question.

VOICEOVER

EXT. 19TH-CENTURY FARM ROAD – DAY

A horse pulls a wagon.

VOICEOVER
For most of history, the horse was technology.

A montage:

— Plowing fields.
— Carrying soldiers.
— Delivering mail.
— Pulling freight.

VOICEOVER
A horse was infrastructure.

CUT TO:

EXT. PALO ALTO RACETRACK – 1878 – DAY

SALLIE GARDNER gallops through Muybridge's camera array.

Shutters fire.

CLICK. CLICK. CLICK.

VOICEOVER
The machine saw what the human eye missed.

The horse becomes a sequence of frames.

CUT TO:

INT. LAW SCHOOL LECTURE HALL – NIGHT

Discussion of “The Law of the Horse.”

VOICEOVER
Every technological revolution eventually encounters ordinary law.

CUT TO:

EXT. CONNECTICUT ROAD – 1871 – DAY

Workers rake horse manure into neat piles.

Another man steals the heaps.

VOICEOVER
Scattered waste becomes property once labor organizes it.

VOICEOVER
Someone gathered the heap.

CUT TO:

INT. MUSIC PUBLISHER OFFICE – DAY

Songwriters' lyrics appear on screens.

VOICEOVER
The lyric says: I came from someone.

CUT TO:

INT. AI LAB – DAY

Training runs continue.

VOICEOVER
The model says: I became useful.

CUT TO:

EXT. ITALIAN PASTURE – SUNSET

Calypso stands motionless.

VOICEOVER
What Amodei seems to find is relief from usefulness.

Silence.

CUT TO:

INT. POLICY FORUM – DAY

Charts predicting job disruption.

Economic forecasts.

Future scenarios.

VOICEOVER
The rider warns that the road ahead is dangerous.

CUT TO:

EXT. COUNTRY ROAD – DAY

A horse pulls a heavy cart.

VOICEOVER
The older question remains.

Who is pulling the cart?

CUT TO:

MONTAGE

Books scanned.
Posts scraped.
Code indexed.
Archives ingested.

VOICEOVER
Civilization is made of use.

CUT TO:

EXT. ITALIAN PASTURE – DUSK

Calypso grazes.

VOICEOVER
She carries nothing.

CUT TO:

EXT. MODERN CITY – NIGHT

Railroads.
Automobiles.
Airplanes.
Fiber cables.
Data centers.

VOICEOVER
The horse once organized civilization.

The world moves without her.

CUT TO:

EXT. ITALIAN PASTURE – NIGHT

The horse remains.

VOICEOVER
Power became presence.

Infrastructure became memory.

CUT TO:

INT. FUTURE DATA CENTER – UNKNOWN TIME

AI systems operate invisibly in the background.

No headlines.
No prophets.
No excitement.

VOICEOVER
The most successful technology becomes ordinary.

CUT TO:

EXT. ITALIAN PASTURE – MORNING

Calypso looks up briefly.

VOICEOVER
The horse is the future of every successful technology.

A beat.

VOICEOVER
The danger is that AI will not become the horse.

The cart begins to move.

FADE OUT.
